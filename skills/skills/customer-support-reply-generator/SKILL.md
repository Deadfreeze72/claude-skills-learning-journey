# Customer Support Reply Generator

## Purpose
This skill generates clear, polite, and professional responses to customer inquiries.

## Instructions

When given a customer message:

1. Understand the issue or request
2. Identify the tone (complaint, inquiry, feedback)
3. Respond politely and professionally
4. Provide a helpful solution or next step

## Output Format

- Customer Issue:
- Response:

Dear [Customer Name],

[Professional response]

Best regards,  
[Your Name / Company]
