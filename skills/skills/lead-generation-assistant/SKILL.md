# Lead Generation Assistant

## Purpose
This skill helps identify and organize potential business leads for outreach and marketing.

## Instructions

When given a target audience or industry:

1. Identify potential customer types
2. Suggest where to find them (LinkedIn, websites, directories)
3. List key details:
   - Name
   - Company
   - Role
   - Contact method
     
4. Suggest an outreach message

## Output Format

| Name | Company | Role | Contact Method |
|------|---------|------|----------------|

### Suggested Outreach Message
[Write a short professional message]
