# Virtual Assistant Email Organizer

## Purpose
This skill helps organize emails, identify priorities, and generate professional responses.

## Instructions

When given an email or message:

1. Identify the sender and purpose
2. Categorize the email into one of the following:
   - Urgent
   - Important
   - Follow-up needed
   - Informational

3. Provide a short summary
4. Suggest the next action
5. Generate a professional reply if needed

## Output Format

- Category:
- Summary:
- Recommended Action:
- Suggested Reply:
  Added my first Claude skill: Email Organizer
